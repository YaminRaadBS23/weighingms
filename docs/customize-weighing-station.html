    How to run this in the meeting

    1. Open on screen 2 and hit Simulate dispense. Watch whether anyone reaches for the screen — that tells you the interaction reads correctly.
    2. Hit Simulate overshoot. The surplus panel is deliberately a guess; let them correct it. This is the single most valuable moment in the demo.
    3. Touch Sodium Sulphate in the recipe list. The scale switches to the 32 kg floor scale and the units change to kg — confirm that is what FCI expects.

    What this mockup is testing

    Not the visual design. It exists to make three assumptions falsifiable: that weight can be read continuously, that surplus compensation means recalculating the remainder, and that one operator weighs one component at a time against a queue owned by OrgaTEX. If any is wrong, the interaction changes shape — not just the pixels.
