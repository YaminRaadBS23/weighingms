## Defect found by the tests and fixed
`EXCEPTION  Yellow RGB  OVERRIDDEN` (two spaces) is 33 characters. The printed line was cut to `OVERRIDD~` while the stored verdict kept the full text, so the structured ticket and the printed ticket disagreed. Fixed in `ticket-composer.ts`: single spaces (`EXCEPTION Yellow RGB OVERRIDDEN`, 31 characters) and the verdict is cut once, so the stored verdict and the printed line are the same string. The model and design text were corrected to match.
